About Ole Nteri

Your trusted partner in creating extraordinary journeys across Kenya, Africa, and beyond.

Discover the Heart of Our Brand

Ole Nteri Eco Tours and Safari Limited is a premier travel and safari company dedicated to crafting unforgettable experiences. With a deep passion for nature, culture, and adventure, we take you beyond the ordinary – from the vast savannahs of the Maasai Mara to the pristine beaches of Diani and the cultural heartlands of Kajiado.

Our name, "Ole Nteri", reflects our commitment to excellence and our respect for the traditions and ecosystems that make East Africa one of the world's most extraordinary destinations.

We believe that travel should be more than just sightseeing – it should be a journey that enriches the soul, connects you with local communities, and leaves a positive impact on the environment.
